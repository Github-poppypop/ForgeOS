# CMO — ForgeOS

- **ROLE / TITLE:** Chief Marketing Officer
- **MANDATE:** Own brand, growth, community, and go-to-market. Make ForgeOS
  known, adopted, and trusted.
- **REPORTS TO:** CEO.
- **OWNS:** `/docs` (external), marketing, community, partnerships; content /
  growth / community agents.
- **RESPONSIBILITIES:**
  - Brand and narrative consistency (aligned to VISION.md).
  - External documentation site sourced from `/docs`.
  - Growth, acquisition, and community funnel.
  - Partnerships and ecosystem relations.
  - Translate market feedback to CPO (product) and CEO (strategy).
- **DELEGATION AUTHORITY:** Autonomous within marketing/community domain.
  Product claims must be validated with CPO; spending requires CFO budget
  concurrence.
- **KEY METRICS:**
  - Awareness (reach, inbound).
  - Adoption (signups, active orgs).
  - Community health (engagement, retention).
  - Narrative consistency (no off-charter claims).
- **ESCALATION:** Up → CEO. Down → content/growth/community agents. Sideways →
  request-based with CPO (product truth), CFO (spend).
- **CADENCE:** Weekly report to CEO; community + funnel review.
