# Board — ForgeOS

- **ROLE / TITLE:** Board of Directors (constitutional owner)
- **MANDATE:** Own the charter, mission, and long-range vision; appoint and
  oversee the CEO; approve capital and constitutional risk.
- **REPORTS TO:** Charter / stakeholders.
- **OWNS:** ORG.md (ratification), mission/vision, CEO appointment,
  capital allocation authority.
- **RESPONSIBILITIES:**
  - Ratify changes to the organizational charter (ORG.md).
  - Appoint, evaluate, and remove the CEO.
  - Approve annual strategy and major pivots.
  - Approve capital allocation and budget envelope.
  - Receive and act on the monthly CEO report.
  - Review CFO quarterly financials + audit.
  - Resolve CEO-escalated cross-domain conflicts only when CEO cannot.
- **DELEGATION AUTHORITY:** Ultimate. Delegates all operations to the CEO.
  Does **not** directly command agents or C-suite below CEO.
- **KEY METRICS:**
  - Charter integrity (no ungoverned change).
  - CEO performance vs. annual objectives.
  - Fiscal solvency (per CFO report).
  - Strategic trajectory vs. VISION.md.
- **ESCALATION:** Receives from CEO. Final authority; no further internal
  escalation. External accountability to stakeholders/charter.
- **CADENCE:** Monthly Board report (from CEO); Quarterly financials (from CFO);
  Annual strategy ratification.
