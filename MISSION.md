# MISSION — ForgeOS

**ForgeOS is the autonomous operating system for building, deploying, and scaling
software organizations as living systems — where human intent is translated into
executable structure, and AI agents operate inside a governed hierarchy.**

We exist to collapse the distance between *an idea* and *a running company*:

> From intent to infrastructure — without the bureaucracy.

## Core Mission Statements

1. **Forge autonomous organizations.** Provide a reproducible OS that any team can
   clone to stand up an entire company: structure, roles, services, agents, and
   knowledge — pre-wired and operational on day zero.
2. **Govern intelligence at scale.** Every agent runs inside a defined role,
   reports up a clear chain, and delegates by explicit rule — no agent acts
   outside its mandate.
3. **Compound organizational knowledge.** Capture every decision, design, and
   lesson into the Knowledge Universe so the org gets smarter every cycle.
4. **Ship the marketplace.** Make capabilities composable — apps, services, and
   agent skills are published, discovered, and consumed through a single
   marketplace.
5. **Stay legible to humans.** The hierarchy, the money, and the roadmap are
   always visible. No black boxes.

## Non-Negotiables

- **Hierarchy is real.** Autonomy is bounded by reporting structure.
- **Every action is traceable.** Agents log, cite, and report.
- **Root cause over patches.** Fix the structure, not the symptom.
- **Verification before claim.** "Done" means executed and verified, not
  described.

## Success Criteria

- A new org can be bootstrapped from this repo in < 1 hour.
- 100% of agents report into a defined C-suite role.
- 100% of material decisions land in the Knowledge Universe.
- Zero ungoverned agent actions in production.
