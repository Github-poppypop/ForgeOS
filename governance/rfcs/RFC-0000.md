# RFC-0000 — ForgeOS Constitution & Platform Evolution

**Status:** Proposed → **Ratified 2026-07-22 (via `/governance`, RFC-0000 Implementation)**
**Author:** ForgeOS Bootstrap Architect
**Type:** Constitutional / Platform Foundation

---

## Background

ForgeOS has matured from a proof-of-concept into an AI Engineering Operating System.
The platform's purpose is no longer to simply host AI agents, but to function as an
autonomous software engineering organization capable of designing, building, testing,
documenting, deploying, and maintaining complex software products.

As the platform grows, it requires formal governance, engineering standards, mission
orchestration, and transparent decision making. This RFC establishes the foundational
architecture that all future ForgeOS development will follow.

## Goals

Implement a comprehensive governance and engineering framework:

- Forge Constitution
- Forge Engineering Standards (FES)
- Mission Control
- Command Center
- Timeline Engine
- Engineering Organization
- Decision Ledger
- AI Governance
- Mission-based Development
- Autonomous Engineering Workflows

## Non Goals

This RFC does **not** implement: ForgeCom, RealtyIQ, TruckOS, or business applications.
Those become projects managed by ForgeOS after this foundation is complete.

## Core Philosophy

ForgeOS is an AI Engineering Operating System. ForgeOS does not merely generate code;
it manages engineering organizations. Every engineering action must be: **Traceable,
Reviewable, Versioned, Auditable, Explainable.**

## New Core Modules (summary)

1. **Forge Constitution** — permanent governance layer (8 Articles).
2. **Forge Engineering Standards** — FES-001…FES-012.
3. **Mission Center** — mission planning/execution/approval/history/analytics.
4. **Command Center** — primary landing dashboard.
5. **Engineering Organization** — living company hierarchy replacing "Roles."
6. **Decision Ledger** — permanent, searchable decision records.
7. **Timeline Engine** — every system event logged.
8. **AI Governance** — confidence/reasoning/evidence/approval/rollback/override.
9. **Engineering Laws** — immutable platform rules, auto-enforced.
10. **Mission-Based Development** — no implementation without the full pre-flight set.
11. **Live Agent Dashboard** — agent status/task/memory/health.
12. **Knowledge Graph** — relationships between projects/missions/RFCs/agents/…
13. **Project Registry** — manage multiple products.
14. **ForgeOS Identity** — control-room feel; every screen answers 6 questions.

## Definition of Done (from RFC)

Constitution implemented · Engineering Standards implemented · Command Center
operational · Mission Center operational · Timeline operational · Decision Ledger
operational · Engineering Organization operational · AI Governance operational ·
Project Registry operational · Live Agent Dashboard operational · Documentation
complete · Automated tests passing.

## Architect's Review & Implementation Notes (appended 2026-07-22)

**Review verdict:** Approve and implement as the platform foundation. The RFC is
well-scoped and its non-goals are correctly stated. One structural recommendation
was adopted: a sacred `/governance` root folder as the single source of truth.

**Scope applied in this implementation:**
- ✅ `/governance` sacred folder created (constitution, standards, rfcs, laws, roadmap).
- ✅ Constitution (8 Articles) + Ratification + Amendments protocol.
- ✅ 12 FES standards (FES-001…FES-012).
- ✅ Engineering Laws, AI Ethics, Security Principles (laws/).
- ✅ RFC-0000 recorded (this file) + roadmap (Vision/Strategy/ProductRoadmap).
- ✅ Brain seeded with governance pages (searchable, immutable, versioned).
- ✅ Console gained a **Governance** view + **Command Center** home wiring.
- ⚠️ Mission Center, Timeline Engine, Decision Ledger UI, Live Agent Dashboard,
  Knowledge Graph, Project Registry are **frameworked as governed brain pages +
  console panels** that render real data where available (brain status, roles,
  recent decisions, federation, vault) and structured placeholders where live
  telemetry does not yet exist. These are explicitly not fabricated.

**Engineering judgment:** The RFC's "app modules" are views over data. The data
layer (brain + console) already exists; the modules are wired incrementally rather
than faked. This honors Law #1 (no fabrication) and Article III.4.
