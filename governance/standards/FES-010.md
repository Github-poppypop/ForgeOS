# FES-010 — Deployment Standards

**Version:** 1.0.0 · **Owner:** DevOps · **Inherit:** all projects

1. No deployment without passing QA. (Law #1)
2. Deployments are automated and repeatable (CI/CD pipelines).
3. Every deployment is tagged and reversible (rollback path).
4. Environments (dev/staging/prod) are separated; prod secrets isolated.
5. Health checks and post-deploy verification are required.
6. Deployment status is reported to the Command Center.
