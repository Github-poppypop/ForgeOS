# ForgeOS Vision

ForgeOS is the control room of an autonomous AI engineering organization. It governs
the full lifecycle of complex software products — from vision to deployment — with
traceability, review, and auditability as first-class concepts. Where typical AI
coding tools generate code, ForgeOS governs an engineering company.

We believe the differentiator is not generation speed but **governance, traceability,
and autonomous execution** done right: every decision recorded, every artifact
versioned, every AI action explainable, every product isolated.
