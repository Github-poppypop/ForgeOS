# ForgeOS Strategy

**Pillar 1 — Governance first.** `/governance` is sacred and supreme. Constitution,
laws, and standards are enforced automatically.

**Pillar 2 — Mission-based development.** No code without an RFC, architecture, and
Definition of Done. Missions are the unit of work.

**Pillar 3 — Isolation by design.** Root brain isolated; app-child brains isolated;
no lateral mingle. Products are governed, never entangled.

**Pillar 4 — Explainable autonomy.** Agents act with confidence/reasoning/evidence
and a human-override path; they never fabricate.

**Pillar 5 — Local-first where quality holds.** Embeddings and core brain run local
(Ollama) to keep the platform private and free of cloud-key coupling.

**Sequence:** (1) Foundation [this RFC] → (2) Mission Center + Timeline + Decision
Ledger operational → (3) Project Registry + Multi-Agent → (4) ForgeCom and the
product lines (RealtyIQ, TruckOS, FreedomOS) as governed projects.
