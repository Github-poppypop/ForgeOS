  usable agent or API key. The driver performs improvements directly instead.
- Alerting is a silent no-op until `SENTRY_DSN` or `ALERT_WEBHOOK_URL` env is set.
- `apps/poolleague` shows as a dirty/untracked entry in `git status` but is not
  present on disk; left uncommitted intentionally (unrelated local artifact).
